Privacy Policy

Iron Sharpens Iron

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Iron Sharpens Iron ("we," "our," or "the app") handles information when you use our Christian apologetics and discipleship application.

1. Information and App Progress

The app includes learning content and features such as lesson completion, streaks, favorites, and personal progress. These features may store information on your device so the app can remember your activity. Information stored locally may be lost if you clear app data or uninstall the app.

The app's current behavior and any information stored or transmitted by third-party software must be considered when determining exactly what information is collected. We will update this policy if our data practices change.

2. Sharing Content

The app may let you share apologetics content using other apps or services on your device. If you choose to share content, the information you send is handled by the sharing destination according to its own privacy practices. Do not share personal information unless you intend to disclose it.

3. Third-Party Services

The app may rely on third-party services to build, distribute, operate, or improve the app. Those services may process technical information as described in their own privacy policies. The specific services used by the released version of the app, and the information they process, should be confirmed before release.

Google Play and Apple may separately process information in connection with app distribution and platform services. Their privacy policies apply to their respective services.

4. Advertising and Purchases

This policy must be updated if advertising, analytics, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or other monetization services are introduced. Any such services will be described here once their data practices have been confirmed.

5. Children's Privacy

Iron Sharpens Iron is a Christian educational application. The app is not intended to collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a manner that raises a privacy concern, please contact us so we can review the situation.

6. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information within our control. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Information retained on your device may remain there until it is deleted through the app, device settings, or removal of the app, as applicable.

7. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You can control certain information through your device settings and your use of the app. Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. Contact us to ask a privacy-related question or make a request.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app's features, services, or legal obligations change. The current version will be published at this webpage, with its effective date updated when appropriate.

9. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Iron Sharpens Iron developer using the developer contact information listed on the app's Google Play or Apple App Store listing.
